# Frozen B7 period and format holdout protocol

Part of issue #17. This extension addresses its explicit diversity/period
holdout requirement; it does not fit production weights or accept Gate B.
Freeze this protocol and cohort before acquiring new source content.

The twelve 2023–2024 works are a convenience selection: four live-action
features, four animated features and four shorts. The shorts include live
action, animation and documentary. Selection uses published film/award context,
not observed critic-list contents or scores. Do not replace a case because its
article lacks useful evidence. These works are held out from the 2008 pilot.

Acquire revision-recorded Wikipedia article text with response time, status,
bytes, hash and CC BY-SA attribution; this carrier is derivative evidence,
not an independent critic. Identify the correct article using title/director/
year/type context; preserve aliases and contradictory dates. No media or
restricted publisher text is copied. All project caches remain in this worktree.

Transcribe only source-supported named-critic year-end selections and published
ranks with exact locators. No inferred rank from prose order, invented critic
identity, or membership inferred from a merely positive review. Preserve source
family and cited original publication. Unknown lists and exposure stay unknown.
Deduplicate known people per film using the existing measure() baseline.
Hold out the entire New Yorker family, including new-period writers, globally
from training; do not merely reuse the two 2008 writers' names as a family filter.
Compare count and reciprocal-rank baselines with observed held-out memberships/
ranks only on supported overlaps. Report ties, denominators and unknowns.

A separate automated reviewer must audit source text without seeing extracted
rows or scores. Retain its corrections and timing boundaries. Report reception
measures only if acquired, in original units with sample sizes and dates. Do not
turn categorical audience grades into numeric votes. The absent full weighted
formula remains a legitimate inconclusive result if components are unavailable.
No tuning, cohort replacement or new normalization on these held-out outcomes.

Report coverage and conditional disagreements by period, assigned format and
source-reported geography; overlapping country memberships are nonadditive.
Sparse shorts with no observed ballots remain visible rather than becoming
negative relevance judgments. This small retrospective study cannot establish
calibration, predictive quality, broad stability or viewing satisfaction.
