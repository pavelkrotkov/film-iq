# B5: Long-tail viewing leads

**Version:** 0.1, research in progress. **Checked:** 2026-10-29 UTC. **Issue:** [#15](https://github.com/pavelkrotkov/film-iq/issues/15). **Context:** [plan §7](../../../plan.md) and [ADR 0008](../../adr/0008-availability-providers.md), [ADR 0011](../../adr/0011-cost-rights.md).

This convenience sample investigates bounded searches for named films. It is not a provider coverage estimate, a complete survey, or an assertion that any title is playable in the US. Public page text and search-index snapshots were checked; no video was played and no library or streaming account was used. A blocked or partial response is **unknown**, not evidence that a film is unavailable. The sample is independent of the pending #12 and #14 cohorts.

## Minimal search and verification

1. Start from a finite prioritized candidate list; search the exact title, director and year against named first-party film, distributor, archive or library sources. Do not search the entire video catalog.
2. Compare title and aliases, director, production/premiere year and stated duration. Record conflicts and keep the match unresolved when they cannot be reconciled.
3. Record the uploader or source, evidence of authority, and whether the source describes a full film, another cut, a trailer/excerpt or an incomplete or unknown copy. A title or uploader name alone is not proof of authorization.
4. Record lookup time, retrieval method and outcome; separately record region, whether a participating account or library is needed, and any stated expiry. Mark US playability and personal entitlement **unknown** until checked by the viewer on the specific account/plan. Never infer removal from a failed or partial lookup.
5. Keep a source link and concise observations only where permitted; do not copy media or protected catalog datasets. Check each provider's terms and retention before automation or persistent observations.

## Recommendation and outstanding evidence

Use manual first-party links and targeted verification for the shortlist. The sample contains promising leads but no measured comparison with TMDB/JustWatch or Movie of the Night, so it cannot show which source fills a real coverage gap. Do not add a general video crawler or provider adapter until a repeated, rights-permitted use case demonstrates benefit. Follow up on actual US playback, account/library eligibility, first-party offer classification and matched #12/#14 gaps; keep unsupported cases on the unavailable-film radar as unknown.
