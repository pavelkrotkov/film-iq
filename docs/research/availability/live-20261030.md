# B4: first TMDB/JustWatch US observation, 2026-10-30

[Issue #14](https://github.com/pavelkrotkov/film-iq/issues/14) remains open.
This is one dated observation of a convenience cohort, not a provider comparison,
validated playback result, accepted Gate B or three-date experiment.

## Identity and acquisition boundary

All 60 source-audited works were searched through TMDB API v3 without a release-year
filter. Searches were bounded to five pages per query; normalized title and
source-year proximity proposed at most three candidate details for review.
Retrieval rank did not accept an identity. A separate automated reviewer compared
all 66 proposed detail records with published title/director/format/date context
and accepted one work identity for each of the 60 candidates. This was neither
human adjudication nor an independent precision benchmark. IMDb identifiers
returned by TMDB were not independently verified or activated.

The reviewer retained conflicting source assertions, names, dates and durations.
For example, Jeanne Dielman has 1975 source context versus TMDB's 1976 release;
Bobi Wine and To Kill a Tiger have 2022 source contexts versus 2023 TMDB releases;
Volcelest, Rrugës and The Man Who Could Not Remain Silent also have different
source-year/release-date assertions. Accepted here means sufficiently identified
**work for offer lookup**, not a reconciled dated event or exact cut. B2's stricter
unresolved-date decision for The Man Who Could Not Remain Silent is not contradicted
or silently converted into precision truth by this offer-specific acceptance.

Searches for Joker, The River, Flow and Tea exceeded the page budget. Their
candidate sets remain incomplete even though the observed title/director evidence
supported the particular work IDs. The other 56 searches completed; none of these
results establishes catalog absence or uniqueness. Competing same-title candidates
were retained rather than discarded. The committed original cohort still has null
activation fields. A separately hashed, ignored local manifest activates only the
reviewed TMDB IDs and must be reused unchanged on subsequent dates.

204 buy, 8 ad-supported and 18 free** observations. Multiple services and access
types for a film are separate rows, not additional films or verified purchases.
Every account entitlement remains unknown; no add-on classification was inferred.

The three complete Shorts responses were Perfectly a Strangeness, Tea and Amarela,
each with one free observation. Seven follow-up diagnostic requests confirmed that
Volcelest, Ootidė, Sanki Yoxsan, Les Belles Cicatrices, Rrugës, Across the Waters and
The Man Who Could Not Remain Silent lacked an explicit US object. In those later diagnostic responses, five had empty global results and two had
only other regions. The original seven schema-failure observations did not retain
response bodies or hashes, so the later receipts cannot prove byte-identical
first-pass payloads or retroactively establish their exact failure causes. These are **unknown US observations**,
not proof that a film is unavailable in the US, a catalog miss or a removal. The
follow-ups occurred minutes later on the same date and do not count as a second
observation date. No removal events were generated.

## Bounded first-party checks

Three public first-party title pages were inspected through a web reader. These
may be crawled snapshots; they are not direct HTTP receipts, playback, signed-in
account checks or confirmation of present US transactions.

- [Netflix's Nimona page](https://www.netflix.com/title/81444554) showed the 2023 title
  and sign-in/join surface, consistent with TMDB catalog presence. Generic
  Standard with Ads marketing does not establish this title's ad-tier entitlement.
- [Disney+'s Inside Out 2 page](https://www.disneyplus.com/browse/entity-ec9f9fa3-fbae-4a25-b722-12c3b8ab0ef4)
  named the 2024 film and Kelsey Mann, consistent with TMDB's Disney Plus listing.
  Its 103-minute duration differs from the [Disney Movies page's](https://movies.disney.com/inside-out-2)
  96 minutes; version/context remains unresolved. Login and plans were visible,
  without account or ad-tier verification.
- [Apple's US No Other Land page](https://tv.apple.com/us/movie/no-other-land/umc.cmc.74yuh67fhbawv4o96mks0y1y2)
  supported title presence, but the reader exposed no transaction button or price.
  TMDB's rent/buy categories were not independently verified. Apple's 95 minutes
  versus TMDB's 92 remain unresolved.

One additional TMDB service-label request identified the selected Netflix, Netflix
Standard with Ads and Disney Plus service labels used in these comparisons. A
service label is not a personal subscription entitlement.

## Requests, costs and timing

The successful B4 API work totals **225 requests**: 91 search, 66 detail, 60 first
US offers, seven diagnostics and one service-label request. All returned HTTP 200;
none exposed quota headers. This does not prove unlimited quota or zero cost.
An earlier restricted-network pass recorded 60 URLError attempts with no verified
upstream request. Those attempts, the parent's separate authentication probe and
unmetered underlying web-reader requests are excluded from 225, explicitly rather
than silently omitted. No raw API responses, images or credentials were retained.

Catalog acquisition processed 2,677,996 response bytes. Its recorded request/body
processing sum, including hashing, JSON decoding and receipt construction, was
**13.971862 seconds**; acquisition wall time was **31.197999
seconds**, including 0.1-second pacing per request and local processing. The offer
pass separately measured request/status/body-read time of **5.172321 seconds**;
its **11.577121-second** full run included pacing and normalization. These timing
boundaries differ and are not a controlled comparison or complete budget estimate.
Seven diagnostics summed 0.501508 seconds and the service-label request 0.092996
seconds at their own request/body-read boundary. Audit/model overhead, web-reader
requests and human labor were not measured. Human review time and monetary cost
remain unknown.
