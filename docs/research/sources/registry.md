# Critical source registry and rights audit

**Version:** 0.1 · **Checked:** 2026-10-29 · **Issue:** [#11](https://github.com/pavelkrotkov/film-iq/issues/11)

This is a source-prioritization audit, not a licensed corpus or proof that an
adapter can ingest a source. The cited examples are edition-specific; they do
not establish coverage or eligibility for every edition. No source text or
ballot is stored here. This report supports [plan §4](../../../plan.md#4-source-universe-and-acquisition),
[ADR 0004](../../adr/0004-typed-provenance.md) and
[ADR 0011](../../adr/0011-cost-rights.md).

The check date records our review, not the time every source was fetched:
some evidence came from cached search results, and access can change.

**Priority** ranks research value, not permission to scrape: **1** is an
important original source or diversity stratum to investigate first; **2**
is useful complementary evidence; **3** is a discovery lead or derivative
aggregate. For every item, permission for the proposed acquisition method,
retention, redistribution and publication must be resolved separately.
Unknown permission is not approval.

BFI and Cannes state restrictions on hyperlinks. Their original document URLs
and terms are given in code formatting as identifiers rather than active
hyperlinks pending permission; this reports their terms, not a legal opinion
about enforceability. Other links below are citations, not a grant to copy.

## Lineage and rank checks

- Ryan Swen's first ten ranked titles and order coincide in the 2024
  [Film Comment](https://www.filmcomment.com/best-films-of-2024-individual-ballots/)
  and [Slant](https://www.slantmagazine.com/features/the-25-best-films-of-2024/)
  individual ballots. Keep both publication records and their context, but
  count the same person/film once when measuring independent critic breadth.
- Amy Taubin selected *Oppenheimer* in both the 2023 BFI and
  [Film Comment](https://www.filmcomment.com/best-films-of-2023-individual-ballots/)
  ballots, with different positions and source eligibility. Preserve each
  original position while recognizing one critic. Do not infer that different
  positions make the person independent.
- A BFI 2023 individual ballot explicitly says its alphabetical list is not
  ranked. The same page displays *Joyland* twice in its film-level section
  with different voters. Treat source rows as observations and reconcile work
  identity; neither display order nor duplicate headings establish rank or
  distinct film identity.
- Separate a critic's ballot from the publisher's aggregate, an aggregator's
  copy, and a festival competition selection from an award. Never count a
  derived aggregate as an independent endorsement of the same underlying vote.
  Unpublished ballots, uncertain matches and unverified eligibility remain
  unknown rather than zero.

These are statements of published terms and observations, not an opinion on
legal enforceability. They are not blanket permissions for automated collection
or public fixture publication. Recheck current terms and any written license
before use; no retention period was verified for the items below.

## Next steps and explicit gaps

1. Resolve access and written reuse/retention rights before running adapters or
   committing real-source fixtures. Keep rights for source text separate from
   rights for factual observations, and do not infer either from an OSS license.
2. Audit an edition's full page set, exact list universe, ballots, ties,
   year semantics and category boundaries before treating extraction as
   complete. Page-level evidence above is not a full historical backfill or a
   measured extraction benchmark.
3. Add geographically and linguistically diverse original sources and
   specialist formats after rights and eligibility checks. This list is a
   deliberately small starting registry, not a representative world sample.
4. Compare named people and cited source relationships across publishers
   before calculating independent endorsement breadth. Record unknown identity
   relationships rather than forcing a merge.

No adapter, acquisition run, historical coverage measure, ingestion accuracy,
or legal permission has been verified by this document.
